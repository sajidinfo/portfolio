# **The Mystery of Bhilwara**

## **Chapter 1: The Man in the Hat**

This mysterious man has been seen a lot in Bhilwara's antique markets and museums lately. Nobody knows much about him. People only know that he loves art and history. He always keeps the brim of his hat down, so it is hard to see his face clearly.

---

## **Chapter 2: The Story Begins**

My wife Gulista is a young art historian. We are travelling in India. One day, we are walking through an antique market in Bhilwara. I see a collection of beautiful paintings. They catch Gulista's attention immediately.

Before long, Gulista recognises one of the paintings. It is an original work by the legendary English painter, William Turner. But why is a Turner painting for sale in an old antique market?

We decide to speak with the owner of the market stall. The stall owner, Tahir, tells us that a few days ago, a strange man sold him the paintings. He explains that he did not realise they were original works. He also says he did not pay much for them.

Gulista begins to worry. She realises the artworks must be stolen.

Tahir promises the girls that he will contact a friend. This friend is an expert in English art. Tahir will ask him to examine the paintings and verify their authenticity (asliyat). He tells them that if the paintings turn out to be stolen, he will call the police the next day.

Gulista and Sajid decide to take Tahir at his word. They leave the market and promise to return the next day. But as they leave, both of them have a feeling that something is not right.

One day, they get a letter from the royal family.

**Sajid:** The royal family!

**Gulista:** What do they want?

**Sajid:** They want us to come to England.

**Gulista:** England!

**Sajid:** Yes. They want to show us the National Gallery in London.

**Sajid:** Let's go.

**Gulista:** Yes. Let's go.

Sajid and Gulista live happily in Bhilwara. They solve mysteries. They help people. They are happy.

**Sajid:** We have a good life.

**Gulista:** Yes. We do.
